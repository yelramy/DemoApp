# 13 — Launch and growth

## Pre-launch checklist
- [ ] Partner MSA signed + pilot rates
- [ ] Whish merchant live
- [ ] Terms/Privacy/Refund/Prohibited published
- [ ] Banned list aligned with partner
- [ ] 10 blind test parcels
- [ ] Support WhatsApp number + macros
- [ ] COD cash process documented
- [ ] Domain + email + brand assets
- [ ] Status page / downtime comms

## Soft launch
1. Friends/family + Instagram close circle
2. Cap weekly orders (e.g. 20) until SLAs green
3. Whish-prepay preferred; COD limited
4. Categories limited (fashion, beauty, supplements, accessories) — no phones/laptops first if theft/customs risk high

## Channels
- Instagram/TikTok explainers (English + spoken Arabic OK in content even if UI English)
- Diaspora groups (pay-for-family angle)
- University/WhatsApp communities
- SEO guides
- Referral credits

## Positioning ads angle
“Final price in USD. Pay Whish. Track to your door.”  
Not “cheapest shipping in Lebanon.”

## Support hours
- Soft launch: extended evenings Asia/Beirut
- Steady: published SLA response <2h daytime

## KPI weekly review
Conversion quote→pay, margin, delivery success, tickets/order, partner scan lag, COD fail rate.
