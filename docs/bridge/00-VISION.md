# 00 — Vision

## One-liner
**Bridge** — Shop the world. Pay with Whish. Delivered in Lebanon.

## Problem
People in Lebanon can browse global stores but often cannot:
1. Pay with local cards on foreign sites
2. Get delivery to a Lebanese address
3. Predict final cost (shipping + customs)
4. Trust WhatsApp-only middlemen with money and packages

Diaspora relatives already send remittances; buying useful goods for family is harder than sending cash.

## Solution
A full English web product that:
- Accepts any supported product link (or catalog browse)
- Returns an **all-in USD quote** (item + fees + shipping + customs estimate)
- Takes payment via **Whish first**, plus COD / OMT / diaspora card pay
- Buys + ships via **logistics partners** (UAE hub first, then US/TR)
- Shows door-to-door tracking and proactive exception handling
- Supports **split checkout**: payer abroad, recipient in Lebanon

## Brand rules
- Audience: Lebanon (and Lebanese diaspora paying in)
- Language: **English only** for v1 product UI (Arabic support later optional)
- Currency display: **USD (fresh)** everywhere
- Tone: clear, calm, trustworthy — not hype, not purple-gradient SaaS cliché
- Hero job: paste link → see final price → pay → track

## Product principles
1. **Landed price is sacred** — no surprise fees after confirm (except true customs variance rules disclosed upfront)
2. **Local rails first** — Whish > COD > OMT > foreign card
3. **Partner for pipes, own the customer** — never outsource brand/support relationship
4. **Automate the happy path; staff the exceptions**
5. **Narrow SKUs beat infinite chaos** — start with allowed categories, expand deliberately

## Success metrics
| Horizon | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Soft launch | Successful delivered orders | 50 |
| Month 3 | Weekly orders | 40+ |
| Month 3 | Quote→pay conversion | ≥25% |
| Month 3 | Delivery success (no loss) | ≥97% |
| Month 3 | Support touches / order | ≤1.5 |
| Month 6 | Gross margin on service layer | 15–20% |
| Month 6 | Founder ops hours / week | ≤5 (with part-time ops) |

## Non-goals (forever or long-term)
- Owning aircraft / primary customs brokerage day one
- Becoming a general marketplace for Lebanese merchants
- Crypto payments in v1
- Arabic-first UI in v1
- Guaranteeing every website on earth
