# 10 — Security, compliance, risk

## Fraud & abuse
- Device + phone velocity limits
- COD risk scoring (new user, high value, far area, prior refuse)
- High-value KYC (national ID) before buy
- Block disposable emails for diaspora if abused
- Suite address abuse monitoring (unknown inbounds)

## Banned / restricted goods
Maintain living list aligned with:
- Airline DG rules
- Lebanese customs
- Partner bans  
Categories: weapons, illicit drugs, counterfeit, alcohol/tobacco (policy), raw lithium loose batteries, live animals, periishables without cold chain, pharma requiring Rx without process, adult content if partner bans.

Checkout hard-blocks banned; soft-warns restricted.

## Customer money protection
- Do not buy until payment cleared (except explicit corporate terms)
- Prefer weekly partner settlement after measurable events
- Segregate customer wallet credits in ledger
- Clear refund SLAs in Terms

## Legal (Lebanon + UAE ops)
- Company registration entity decision (LB LLC vs foreign)
- Consumer Terms, Privacy (data retention, WhatsApp content)
- Importer of record / customs liability opinion from counsel
- Payment merchant agreements (Whish, PSP)
- Partner MSA + DPA
- Trademark/domain

## Insurance
- Require partner cargo insurance or buy policy on manifests
- Communicate claim caps to users honestly

## Data privacy
- Minimize PII
- OTP logs retention limits
- Right to delete (with accounting retention exceptions)
- Staff access least-privilege + audit

## Operational risk register
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Partner theft/collapse | small float, dual partners, intake photos, MSA |
| Customs seizure | bans list, accurate invoices |
| COD loss | deposits, area limits, courier contracts |
| Parser breakage | manual intake path always available |
| Margin erosion | floor checks, rate card updates |
| Reputational WhatsApp drama | proactive status + refunds playbook |
