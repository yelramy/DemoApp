# 01 — Market and positioning

## Market facts
- Lebanese cards frequently fail on foreign e-commerce.
- COD remains a trust default; Whish is the leading local wallet rail.
- “Buy for me / ship for me” WhatsApp agencies are common and opaque.
- Physical model is universal: **foreign warehouse → consolidate → air/sea to Lebanon → customs → last mile**.
- Shipped.io (productized Amazon + Whish) closed — leaves a UX gap.

## Competitor map
| Type | Examples | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp shoppers | Order2Lebanon, Eve Concierge | Opaque quotes, weak tracking, brand = chat |
| Multi-hub forwarders | PicknShip, iShip, Vuelb | Ops-heavy, not productized payments UX |
| US address forwarders | SkyShip, Boxit4me, MyUS | Payment still hard if user can’t buy abroad |
| Pure Dubai cargo | Zmzm, BBC, ST Dubai | B2B freight, not consumer shopping UX |

## Bridge wedge
1. **Instant all-in quote** (not “wait for WhatsApp reply”)
2. **Whish-native checkout** + COD/OMT
3. **Diaspora pays / Lebanon receives**
4. **Tracking + status honesty**
5. **Partner logistics underneath** (UAE-first)

## Positioning statement
For people in Lebanon who want products from abroad, Bridge is the shopping checkout that turns any link into a final USD price payable with Whish, with delivery to their door — unlike WhatsApp agencies, it is transparent, trackable, and built for local payment reality.
